ℹ More info
Standard subscription covers each minor release (~6 months). Premium subscription additional maintenance extends even minors by 2 years (EUS). Extended Life Cycle, Premium subscription additional maintenance (ELC) adds 6 years from GA on even minors from RHEL 9.2+, 8.10, and 10.2+. Long Life add-on terms extend the last minor of a major (8.10, 9.10, 10.10). RHEL 7 uses the legacy Extended life cycle support (ELS) add-on instead of ELC. Dates are from the errata policy, not the lifecycle API.
